I'm Sorry

Dear Bhonduuu,

Someone whispered poison in my ears, and I was weak enough to believe her over you. She told me stories, made me doubt everything we had, and pushed me to let you go. I broke up with you without ever explaining - and I'm ashamed that I let her steal my voice too.

You never asked me why I was going. Maybe you didn't care enough to ask. Or maybe your heart was already too tired to fight for someone who wouldn't stand up for you. Either way, I let you slip away, and that guilt will stay with me forever.

And now she's gone too. She cheated on me, and she left just like she entered - quietly, like she never mattered. And you know what hurts most? Losing her doesn't hurt. Not even a little. It's you I lost. It was always you.

I know you probably won't come back. I've told myself a thousand times not to hope. But I had to write this down, because I never got to say it to your face. I was a fool chosen by lies, and I threw away the only truth I ever had.

You deserved better. You always did. And I'm sorry, from the deepest, saddest part of me.

Forever yours,
Your foolish boy

A Letter For You

Read this slowly, it's from my heart

My dearest Bhonduuu,

I know you'll probably never read this.

But I wrote it anyway, the way I write everything now - for you.

Someone I should have never trusted

took my hand away from yours.

I didn't fight for you. I didn't even ask why.

I just... walked away. Like you were nothing.

But you were everything. You always were.

The one who lied to me broke me too,

and now I'm here holding only the memory of you.

If wishes were bridges, I'd crawl back to you on my knees.

But I know this page will stay unread,

and my heart will stay broken,

because I know she will not come - you will not come.

Still, one day I hope you know the truth. ♥
